📖 Glossary — for your viva

FI Number — the corpus you need so that withdrawing 4%/year covers your annual spending forever. Estimated as 25 × annual spending.

Rule of 72 — divide 72 by your annual return rate to get a quick estimate of how many years it takes your money to double.

Effective Tax Rate — total tax paid as a percentage of gross income (different from your tax slab rate, which only applies to the last rupee earned).

Savings Rate — the share of your take-home income you actually save or invest, the single biggest driver of how fast you reach FI.

Real vs Nominal Return — nominal return is the raw growth rate; real return subtracts inflation to show growth in today's purchasing power.

Emergency Fund — how many months of expenses your current savings could cover if income stopped; 3–6 months is a common safety target.

Financial Health Score — a 0–100 composite of savings rate, emergency fund coverage, and how much of your surplus is invested for growth.

Compound Growth — each year's investment gains are added to the principal, so future gains are earned on a larger base — the engine behind the wealth chart.
